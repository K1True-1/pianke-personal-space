The feeling is quiet solitude, openness, stillness, fresh air, very soothing at first glance. 80 percent of the composition is open negative space: smooth overcast sky and glassy water meeting almost invisibly, delicate water ripples only in the lower quarter. Cool silver-blue, soft blue-gray and misty slate midtones with gentle luminosity; do not make the scene dark or black, do not use a dark vignette. No bright white hotspot, strong sun disc, saturated colors or dramatic contrast. The sky is broad, luminous and softly layered with wisps of mist, not stormy. Upper half and entire left half particularly calm so interface text can overlay. Keep the mountains a tiny secondary distant detail, not a prominent foreground peak. Premium minimalist fine-art long-exposure landscape photography, delicate natural atmosphere, clean and unbusy.
